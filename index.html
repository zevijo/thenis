The Problem

Unstructured intake and verification bottlenecks

In regulated workflows — KYB, KYC, tokenisation, claims, litigation, investment finance — chaotic client documentation isn't just inconvenient. It creates real liability exposure.

Unstructured input

  • Documents arrive as PDFs, scans, emails and spreadsheets, with no shared structure
  • Manual review is slow, inconsistent and hard to audit
  • Gap analysis and contradiction checks are done by hand, case by case
  • Findings are rarely source-linked, so provenance is lost the moment a summary is written

Review-ready output

A single, structured, source-linked evidence pack.

  • Every finding is traceable to the document it came from
  • Every deliverable carries a full audit trail
  • Human reviewer sign-off before anything ships

Product Workflow

From intake to review-ready evidence pack

Each AI output is flagged, source-linked and routed for human review before it forms part of a deliverable.

1

Upload mixed files

PDFs, scans, emails and spreadsheets, as they arrive.

2

Ingest and classify

Document parsing, OCR and jurisdiction tagging.

3

PII and privilege check

Redaction and privilege classification before anything is indexed.

4

Build chronology and issues

Entities, authority and timeline extracted from the matter.

5

Detect gaps and contradictions

Risk classifier flags what's missing or inconsistent.

6

Source-linked fact summary

Every finding attributed back to its originating document.

7

Human review and override

Lawyer or compliance escalation with full override rights.

8

Evidence pack export

PDF / DOCX evidence pack and Customer Information File (CIF).
